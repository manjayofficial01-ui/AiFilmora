APP-007 / APP-008

Real frames, real waveforms

Video clips carry filmstrip frames on a power-of-two time grid; audio clips carry an RMS waveform filled from the lane bottom, so silence reads as a thin line and pauses become findable. AiFilmora already extracts frames in probeVideo — the scrub cache is a refactor away.

APP-011 / APP-012 / APP-015

Clip state is visible

Badges for freeze, speed, reversed and gain; a colour-wheel dot plus look name for a grade; a wedge where a transition applies; per-track gain and a level meter with peak-hold in the track header. The timeline stops being a colour chart.

APP-017 / APP-020 / APP-022

The inspector cannot hide a change

Collapsible sections, persisted per title, with an accent dot when a collapsed section has been modified. A reset link appears only when expanded and changed. The AI Mate resolves @playhead, @selected, @in-out and @marker: into concrete times before the model sees them.

APP-001 / APP-013 / APP-014

Honest geometry

Markers use one shape, read and written by the same definition — today the writer stores {t,name} while the renderer reads m.start/m.end, so the flag renders at NaN. The ruler becomes a 20-step ladder and snap thresholds move into pixels so behaviour is zoom-invariant.

APP-023 / APP-024

Automation you can watch

An external agent’s edits are performed on the timeline by a named, colour-coded cursor and land as a single undo step. Nothing mutates the live project until Apply; the preview shows ghost clips. Stop mid-run rolls back.
