# F6: Filmora AI tools and UI entry points

## Findings

### [1] Official Windows User Guide TOC (Filmora 14/15 era) catalogs AI tools under three IA buckets — AI-Powered Video Editing, AI-Powered Audio Editing, and AI-Powered Text Editing — plus export-side AI Thumbnail Creator under Exporting & Share.
- quote: "AI-Powered Video Editing … AI-Powered Audio Editing … AI-Powered Text Editing … AI Thumbnail Creator"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live guide; notes "Filmora's update to Version 15 is now officially live")
- confidence: high

### [2] AI Mate (formerly AI Copilot) is launched from an AI Mate icon on the timeline toolbar and opens a floating chat window with a bottom-left mode dropdown offering Action, Guide, AIGC, Inspiration to Video Agent, and Auto.
- quote: "head to the timeline at the bottom and locate the AI Mate icon, and click on it"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/ai-copilot-editing.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [3] AI Text-Based Editing has four documented UI entry points: startup/project Create Project options ("AI Text Based Editing"), timeline clip right-click > Smart Edit Tool > AI Text Based Editing, timeline toolbar "More" icon > AI Text Based Editing, and top menu Tools > Audio > AI Text Based Editing.
- quote: "Method 1: Using the AI Text-Based from the Main Menu … Method 2: Using the Timeline Dropdown Menu … Method 3: Using the Timeline Toolbar … Method 4: Using the Top Toolbar Option"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/ai-text-based-editing.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (text references Filmora V13 as install baseline)
- confidence: high

### [4] Auto captions / Dynamic Captions live under the Titles menu bar area: Speech-to-Text is Title > Speech to Text, while Dynamic Captions is a left "AI Captions" section beside Titles; both write caption tracks editable via double-click and right-side Basic/Text/Customize inspector panels.
- quote: "click on the \"AI Captions\" section on the left from the \"Titles\" section on the menu bar. Next, access the \"Dynamic Captions\" option from the left"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/auto-caption.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [5] Speech-to-Text is reachable from four places: clip right-click menu, homepage left Toolbox > AI Captions, top menu Tools > Speech to Text, and the timeline toolbar; Text-to-Speech is a left-side "Text to Speech" panel with voice presets and a "Voice Modeling" clone option.
- quote: "After importing the video to the timeline, expand the toolbar above it and select the Speech to Text feature from there."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/stt-tts-srt.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [6] AI Translation (with lip sync) is opened by right-clicking a video in the media library and choosing AI Translation; modes are "Translate Voice and Text" or "Only Translate Text", with Auto Match subtitle alignment; Lip Sync is limited to videos under 5 minutes and only appears for voice+caption translation; 23 source languages, 14 voice/text target languages.
- quote: "Lip Sync is available for videos under 5 minutes and appears only when using voice and caption translation."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/ai-translation.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [7] Silence Detection is a top-menu path: Tools > Audio > Silence Detection, with volume threshold and minimum duration sliders then Analyze and "Finish and Replace".
- quote: "expand the Tools option from the toolbar located at the top. Now, expand the audio option and click on the Silence Detection feature."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/silence-detection.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [8] AI Smart Cutout is an inspector/properties-panel tool: Properties panel on the right > AI Matting (Image or Video section) > Smart Cutout toggle, with Smart Brush, Eraser, Invert, Apply — works for both still images and video clips on the timeline.
- quote: "Head to the properties panel on the right and enable the AI Matting under the Image section. Scroll through the panel and locate the Smart Cutout feature."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/ai-smart-cutout-for-windows.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high
