# F8: Filmora tools and workflows often missing in clones

## Findings

### [1] Filmora's official Windows User Guide (current 14/15-era docs) documents Instant Cutter as a standalone lossless trim tool for HEVC/H.265, reachable from the home-screen "┇" Instant Cutter tab, File > Import Media > Import with Instant Cutter Tool, or Project Media > Import > Import with Instant Cutter Tool.
- quote: "Filmora's Instant Cutter tool is designed for situations when immediate video trimming is necessary. It supports HEVC (H.265) videos and offers lossless editing, allowing you to cut and trim your videos without losing quality."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/import-media-with-instant-cutter.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [2] Screen and webcam recording is present with three entry points (startup-window Screen Recorder; File > Record Media; Media toolbar) offering Record PC Screen, Record Voiceover, and Record from Webcam, with Area/Full Screen/Window modes and camera background blur/removal.
- quote: "Enter the editing interface and click File > Record Media. From the expanded menu, choose between Record PC Screen, Record Voiceover, or Record from Webcam."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/record-pc-screen.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [3] Proxy workflow is present under File > Preferences (Ctrl+Shift+,) > Performance tab, with auto-create when media resolution exceeds 1280x720 or 1920x1080 and optional auto-delete of proxies when a project is closed.
- quote: "Head over to the File menu, and find Preferences option. Alternatively, you can use the shortcut \"CTRL+SHIFT+,\" to bring up the Preferences window and then click on the Performance tab."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/proxy-video-editing.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [4] Silence Detection is present at top-toolbar Tools > Audio > Silence Detection, with volume-threshold and minimum-duration sliders, an Analyze button, and a Finish and Replace action that rewrites the clip.
- quote: "Once you enter the editing interface, expand the Tools option from the toolbar located at the top. Now, expand the audio option and click on the Silence Detection feature."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/silence-detection.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [5] Beat tooling is dual-path: AI Auto Beat Sync lives at startup-screen left toolbox or Tools > Auto Beat Sync (aligns cuts/transitions to music beats), and a separate Detect Beats and Add Beat Markers guide sits under Advanced Audio Editing in the official Windows guide.
- quote: "Start by launching Filmora and accessing Auto Beat Sync from the startup screen in the toolbox on the left. Conversely, you can access the feature by navigating to Tools > Auto Beat Sync from the top menu."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/auto-beat-sync.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [6] Motion Tracking is present in the right-side properties panel under Basic: enable the Motion Tracking toggle, press Click to Start Motion Tracking, then use the Link drop-down to attach text, images, or effects to the tracked subject.
- quote: "Once the video is successfully added, head to the properties panel on the right and enable the Basic section. Locate the Motion Tracking feature and turn its toggle on."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/motion-tracking.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [7] Planar Tracking is present with three UI entries (Video > Basic > Planar Tracking button; AI Tracking Tools icon above the timeline; Tools > Video > Planar Tracking) and two modes: Auto mode and Advanced mode with four adjustable tracking points and optional frame-by-frame analysis.
- quote: "Method 1: Double-click the clip on the timeline to open the editing panel. Navigate to Video > Basic and click the button next to Planar Tracking to activate the feature."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/planar-tracking.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [8] AI Smart Cutout is present for both images and videos at properties panel > AI Matting > Smart Cutout, with Smart Brush selection, Eraser, Invert, and Apply — distinct from AI Portrait Cutout which is listed as its own feature.
- quote: "Head to the properties panel on the right and enable the AI Matting under the Image section. Scroll through the panel and locate the Smart Cutout feature. Turn the toggle on and select Smart Brush."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/ai-smart-cutout-for-windows.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [9] AI Extend (AI Video Extender) is present and extends clips by 5-8 seconds (audio 10-30s) via two entries: the AI Extend icon on the media timeline, or Stock Media > Video Extend with Extend Forward / Extend Backward, duration, resolution, and prompt controls.
- quote: "The AI Extend feature of Filmora intelligently extends clips by 5-8 seconds, maintaining smooth transitions and consistent scenes to preserve story flow."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/ai-extend.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [10] Freeze Frame, Reverse, and speed-ramp (Segmented Speed Control) are all clustered under the clip Speed menu: right-click > Speed > Add Freeze Frame (default 5s freeze via Duration), Video edit panel > Speed tab > Reverse Speed, and right-click > Speed > Add Speed Point for multi-segment Slow/Normal/Fast ramps with Clear Clip Speed reset.
- quote: "Right-click on the video clip in the timeline, select Speed and choose Add Freeze Frame from the menu. Filmora will automatically split the clip and insert a still image at the selected frame."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/freeze-frame.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [11] Crop & Pan & Zoom is present via the Crop icon or right-click > Crop and Zoom, with aspect presets 16:9, 4:3, 9:16, 1:1, 3:4, 21:9, 4:5 plus custom W/H; the Pan & Zoom tab uses green Start / red End frames with Zoom In, Zoom Out, Pan Left, Pan Right, and Swap modes.
- quote: "Select a video clip in the timeline, then click the Crop icon to open the Crop and Zoom panel. Alternatively, right-click the clip and choose Crop and Zoom from the menu."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/pan-and-zoom.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [12] Chroma Key, AI Color Palette (upgraded Color Match), and Video Stabilization all live in the right-side properties panel: Chroma Key under AI Matting with color picker and Tolerance; AI Color Palette under Color > Basic with reference-from-Timeline/Local and Protect skin tones; Video Stabilization under Basic with a strength slider.
- quote: "Select the video clip and go to the right-side settings panel. Enable AI Matting under the video options, then switch on the Chroma Key tool. Use the color picker to click on the green background, which will instantly remove it."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/green-screen.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [13] Compound clips and Detach Audio are present as timeline workflows: Tools > Create Compound Clip or right-click > Create Compound Clip (with Save as Compound Clip Custom to Filmora cloud, nested timeline layering, and Detach Compound Clip); Detach Audio is a right-click menu action that splits audio to an independent track.
- quote: "Once the clips are in the timeline panel, select both and navigate towards the top toolbar. From the given tabs, select \"Tools\", and from the dropdown menu, choose \"Create Compound Clip\"."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/compound-clip.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [14] The official Windows guide TOC confirms remaining clone-gap workflows as first-class pages: Screen Recording | Windows, Create Multi-Screen Videos (split screen), Multi-Clip Editing + Select All Clips + Group editing (batch), Creating Projects / startup window / Templates / Import Subprojects / third-party cloud drives, and stock-backed 2.9M+ Creative Assets with a Stock Media panel that hosts AI tools.
- quote: "Filmora User Guide for Windows — Learn how to use Filmora for Windows with step-by-step guides covering setup, projects, media, video, audio, text, effects, export, sharing, and cloud backup."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/
