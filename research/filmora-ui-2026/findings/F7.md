# F7: Filmora icons, theme, density

## Findings

### [1] Filmora supports three interface appearance options — Light, Dark, and System Default — configured at File > Preferences > General (Windows) or Wondershare Filmora > Preferences > General (Mac) under "Interface Theme" / "Appearance".
- quote: "For Windows: Click File > Preferences > General, then you can switch the appearance to Light/Dark mode. For Mac: Click Wondershare Filmora > Preferences > General, then you can change the appearance to Light/Dark mode."
- url: https://support.wondershare.com/how-tos/filmora/how-to-change-the-interface-color.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [2] Theme switching is available only from Filmora 9.0 onward; v8.x and below have no theme customization, and the change applies immediately without restart.
- quote: "Only available in Filmora 9.0 and later … The change takes effect immediately without requiring a restart."
- url: https://support.wondershare.com/how-tos/filmora/how-to-change-the-appearance.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [3] System Default appearance follows the OS skin: "The skin color of the program will be consistent with your operating system if you choose System Default during setup."
- quote: "The skin color of the program will be consistent with your operating system if you choose System Default during setup."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/panel-layout.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [4] Filmora's editor workspace is a four-panel architecture: Property Panel, Player Panel (Preview Window), Media Panel (Media Library), and Timeline Panel.
- quote: "Wondershare Filmora is now equipped with four types of panel layouts … Property Panel … Player Panel … Media Panel … Timeline Panel"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/panel-layout.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [5] Six named workspace layout modes ship in the product: Default, Organize, Edit, Short Video, Classic, and Dual (Dual targets multi-monitor use).
- quote: "There are six layout models to choose from … Default … Organize … Edit … Short Video … Classic … Dual"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/panel-layout.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [6] The primary custom toolbar is a top tab strip whose named tabs are Media, Stock Media, Effects, Stickers, Transitions, Titles, and Templates.
- quote: "navigate upwards and locate a bar where you can find \"Media\", \"Stock Media\", \"Effects\", and other tabs. This is named Filmora's custom toolbar"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/panel-layout.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [7] Timeline density is adjustable: track height is user-selectable via Track Manager > Adjust Track Height, and zoom is controlled by time-ruler drag, a zoom slider, and a "Zoom to Fit Timeline" button in the top-right of the timeline.
- quote: "Open the Track Manager and access the Adjust Track Height option. Choose the height from the options provided. … the Zoom to Fit Timeline button in the top-right corner … also use the zoom slider for manual control."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/use-the-timeline.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [8] Timeline track chrome uses three named icon controls: Eye (hide video track from preview), Speaker (mute audio track), and Lock (block track edits).
- quote: "Click the Eye icon on any video track to hide it from the preview … For audio tracks, click the Speaker icon to mute … click on the Lock icon to lock the track"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/use-the-timeline.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [9] Filmora always places video and audio on separate tracks ("video and audio are always placed on separate tracks to keep the timeline organized"), and the Playhead is the named current-position indicator.
- quote: "In Filmora, video and audio are always placed on separate tracks to keep the timeline organized. … The Playhead is an important indicator in the timeline that shows your current position in the video."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/use-the-timeline.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [10] Export is the sole primary action button in the export dialog; destinations are tabbed as Local / Device / YouTube / TikTok / Vimeo / Burn a DVD, and the in-dialog confirm control is labeled Export.
- quote: "Step 1: Click the Export button and choose the Local tab. … When you're finished adjusting your video, press the Export button in the Export window."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/guide/export-to-local.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium
