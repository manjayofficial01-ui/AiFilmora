# F2: Premiere Pro + Descript — text-based editing and GenAI

## Findings

### [1] Adobe Premiere's Text-Based Editing lets editors create and cut sequences from a timecoded transcript, with transcript edits auto-trimming matching timeline clips.
- quote: "Use Text-Based Editing to create and edit video sequences using a transcript of the video. When you select and rearrange the text, related video clips are automatically trimmed and adjusted in the timeline without losing the context. The transcript includes timecode metadata and syncs dynamically with clips on the timeline."
- url: https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/edit-video-using-text-based-editing/overview-of-text-based-editing.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-01-07
- confidence: high

### [2] Premiere Text-Based Editing uses Adobe Sensei AI for auto-transcription and supports bulk filler-word removal plus per-speaker dialogue deletion.
- quote: "Premiere uses Adobe Sensei's AI for precise auto-transcription of dialogue. Manage filler words and speaker dialogue: Detect and bulk-delete filler words like uh and umm from the transcripts. Filter the dialogue of a specific speaker in the transcript and delete it from the video."
- url: https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/edit-video-using-text-based-editing/overview-of-text-based-editing.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-01-07
- confidence: high

### [3] Premiere v26.5 (September 2026) shipped Paper Edit, which builds a sequence by selecting multiple transcript lines in the Text panel.
- quote: "Build sequences faster with Paper Edit — Create sequences directly from your transcript with Paper Edit. Select multiple lines of text in the Text panel to quickly assemble a sequence from your transcript."
- url: https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/whats-new/whats-new.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-09
- confidence: high

### [4] Premiere Generative Extend (Firefly-powered, cloud) extends only the start or end of a clip (not both), requires internet, and cannot create or extend spoken dialog.
- quote: "Just click and drag to seamlessly add a few more frames to the beginning or end of a video or audio clip... An internet connection is required to access Generative Extend as it utilizes a cloud AI model. Generative Extend cannot create or extend spoken dialog. During extension, existing dialog will be muted... Generative Extend can only be applied to the beginning or the end of a clip, not both."
- url: https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/edit-with-generative-ai/generative-extend-overview.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-09
- confidence: high

### [5] Generative Extend now accepts 360p–4K UHD sources in any aspect ratio plus interlaced/non-square-pixel footage, but outputs extensions at 30 fps max, 8-bit, SDR, mono/stereo only.
- quote: "Source clips can be 12–60 fps, but extensions above 30 fps are generated at 30 fps. Bit Depth: Source clips can be 8-bit, 10-bit, or 16-bit, but extensions are generated in 8-bit. Color Space: Source clips can be SDR or HDR, but extensions are generated in SDR."
- url: https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/edit-with-generative-ai/generative-extend-overview.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-09
- confidence: high

### [6] Premiere v26.5 (September 2026) added the Generative Media Tool: prompt-to-generate video and sound effects that land as editable timeline clips.
- quote: "Generate media with the Generative Media Tool — Generate video and sound effects from text prompts directly in your Premiere timeline. Add the generated media as editable clips to your sequence, so you can continue refining your edit."
- url: https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/whats-new/whats-new.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-09
- confidence: high

### [7] Firefly web/Boards can hand off generated video into Premiere via Edit > Open in Premiere desktop; assets land in a Firefly Downloads bin at original Firefly resolution.
- quote: "Hover your cursor over the generated video and select Edit > Open in Premiere desktop. The media automatically appears in Firefly Downloads bin of the Project panel of your currently open Premiere project. The video transferred to Premiere retains its original resolution and quality from Firefly."

- source_type: primary
- published: unknown

- quote: "Just type out your changes and hit Video regenerate—Descript will make an AI clone of your voice and change your mouth movements so you look and sound like you said it that way the first time. Video regenerate is best for correcting words or short phrases... It's not built to generate entirely new video clips from text."
- url: https://www.descript.com/video-regenerate
