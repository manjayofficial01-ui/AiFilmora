# F1: Filmora deep dive — AI features, UI, positioning

## Findings

### [1] Filmora 15 is the current major desktop release as of mid-2026, marketed as an AI-first consumer/prosumer editor across Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, and iPad.
- quote: "Filmora 15 — Edit as an Expert with Filmora AI. Available on: [Desktop] [Mobile]"
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live site as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [2] Filmora's timeline model is magnetic multi-track with dual timelines, compound clips, and source/timeline dual-monitor preview — layered complexity aimed at prosumers, not full NLE parity.
- quote: "Edit across dual timelines, preview source and sequence together, and stay in full control. Compare, cut, and sync every scene with precision to fit a professional editing flow."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/timeline-video-editor.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live site as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [3] Filmora 15.0.1 (November 2025) was the structural jump that shipped AI Extend, Pen Tool, Animated Charts, dual-timeline editing, Import Subprojects, dual-monitor Source/Timeline preview, and a 32-bit audio pipeline.
- quote: "Dual-Timeline Editing — Manage multiple compound clips through tabs, easily switch between primary and secondary timelines, and enhance editing efficiency for complex projects."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/whats-new-in-filmora-video-editor.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-11
- confidence: high

### [4] Filmora 15.1.0 (December 2025) launched AI Mate, a conversational editing assistant, and natively integrated third-party generative video models Sora 2 and Veo 3.1 into Text-to-Video / Image-to-Video, with Kling 2.5 powering Normal Mode 2.0.
- quote: "Text to Video: Native integration with Sora 2 and Veo 3.1 for cinematic generation. Additionally, Normal Mode 2.0 is now powered by the Kling 2.5 model, delivering significantly enhanced video quality within the existing interface."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/whats-new-in-filmora-video-editor.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-12
- confidence: high

### [5] By mid-2026 Filmora's AI stack is multi-vendor: Seedance 2.0 and GPT Image 2 landed in v15.4.2 (April 2026), Nano Banana 2 in v15.3.0 (March 2026), and Topaz-powered upscaling in v15.2.7 (February 2026).
- quote: "Seedance 2.0 Integration — Now available directly inside Filmora — the advanced Seedance 2.0 AI model powers text-to-video and image-to-video generation."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/whats-new-in-filmora-video-editor.html
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-02 through 2026-04
- confidence: high

### [6] AI Mate is a multi-mode agent (Auto routes to Guide / AIGC / Action / Idea to Video) that can execute multi-step in-timeline edits from natural language, generate assets, and walk beginners through tools — but only controls a subset of Filmora features.
- quote: "Currently, AI Mate is connected to only part of Filmora's features. It focuses on basic tools commonly used by beginners and everyday editors."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/ai-copilot-editing.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live site as of 2026-09; AI Mate first shipped Dec 2025)
- confidence: high

### [7] AI Mate conversation memory is short-lived (only recent days) and the FAQ warns of LLM hallucinations — a productization gap for long-running or multi-week projects.
- quote: "Due to storage limits, AI Mate only keeps conversation data from the recent few days. Older conversations are automatically deleted and will no longer influence future responses."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/ai-copilot-editing.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live site as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [8] Filmora pricing is three individual tiers — Basic (annual), Advanced (US$59.99/yr, list $69.99), and Perpetual (one-time, locked to Filmora 15) — with Advanced/Perpetual including 1,000 AI credits and Basic gating most generative AI behind pay-per-use credits.
- quote: "Advanced — US$59.99 /yr (US$ 69.99). 1,000 Filmora AI Credits/mo. Used for 20+ AI features."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/shop/buy/buy-video-editor.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live pricing as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

### [9] AI credits are a hard metering mechanism: Advanced issues 1,000 credits every 30 days and unused credits are reclaimed; credits are shared across Windows/Mac on the same Wondershare ID; high-speed mode consumes credits while normal mode is slower and sequential.
- quote: "In a subscription plan, Filmora AI credits are issued every 30 days starting from the purchase date. Any unused credits from the previous 30-day period are reclaimed when the new credits are issued."
- url: https://filmora.wondershare.com/shop/buy/buy-video-editor.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (live FAQ as of 2026-09)
- confidence: high

- quote: "You can access all of Filmora's basic editing features for free, and easily export your videos. However, watermarks will be added to the exported files."
